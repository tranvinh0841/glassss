CACH 1 - GitHub tu build (khong can cai gi tren may)
1. Tao tai khoan va 1 repository moi tren github.com (de Private cung duoc).
2. Giai nen file zip nay, keo tha TOAN BO noi dung (ke ca thu muc .github) vao repository.
   Luu y: thu muc .github la thu muc an, phai co trong repository.
3. Vao tab "Actions" > "Build APK" > "Run workflow" (hoac no tu chay sau khi upload).
4. Doi khoang 3-6 phut, bam vao lan chay xong > phan "Artifacts" > tai GlassLauncher-apk.
   Giai nen se ra app-release.apk.

CACH 2 - Android Studio tren may tinh
1. Mo Android Studio > Open > chon thu muc nay, doi Gradle sync xong.
2. Menu Build > Build APK(s). APK nam o app/build/outputs/apk/release/ (hoac debug/).

CAI LEN TIVI
  adb install -r app-release.apk
Dat lam man hinh chinh: bam nut Home tren remote, chon "Glass Launcher" > "Luon luon".
Neu tivi khong hien hop thoai chon, vao Cai dat > Ung dung > Ung dung mac dinh > Man hinh chinh.
Luu y: neu da go cac launcher goc thi dung go launcher dang chay cho den khi Glass Launcher chay on dinh.
